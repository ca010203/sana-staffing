About this data
  • Hours come from Push's Clock Hours (by position) and Schedule Hours reports, pulled 18 Nov 2026 for 1 Jan to 17 Nov. Only reports were run; nothing in Push was changed.
  • Hours are after breaks. Hour-of-day figures spread each shift across the hours it covers, so they total about 0.6% less than the shift totals: a few clocks have no clock-out time.
  • Dollar figures are estimates: clocked hours times the hourly rate set for each job on the Jobs tab. They are not Push payroll numbers, since Push's labour reports give hours only.
  • The hours are a snapshot from the pull date and don't update on their own. Job, side and department settings are saved on this page and shared by everyone who can open it; only editors can change them.
